Dumplings

Description

A Dumpling is a broad culinary category that generally consists of small pieces of dough—made from flour, water, or potatoes—that are wrapped around a savory or sweet filling, or simply cooked as a doughy mass. They can be boiled, steamed, pan-fried, or simmered in broth.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20 is ideal)
  • 1 grated carrot
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1–2 tsp grated ginger
  • 2–3 green onions
  • Mushrooms (optional, but they keep the filling juicy)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Splash of rice vinegar
  • Black pepper, paprika, & pinch of white pepper

For the sauce combine:

  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp chili crisp or chili oil
  • ½ tsp sesame oil
  • Fresh green onions
  • Optional: tiny squeeze of honey

Steps

  1. Light oil in a nonstick pan.
  2. Brown the bottoms for 2–3 minutes.
  3. 3. Add about ¼ cup water (just enough to cover the bottom). .
  4. 4. Cover and steam 6–8 minutes until the water evaporates.
  5. 5. Let the bottoms crisp again for about a minute. .
